
# My Favorite Movies

Here are five of my favorite movies and why I like them.

## 1. The Notebook

The Notebook is a romantic movie about two people who fall in love despite the challenges they face. I like it because it shows how love and memories can last through time.

## 2. Cosmic Princess Kaguya!

Cosmic Princess Kaguya! is an animated movie inspired by the Japanese folktale of Princess Kaguya. I like its beautiful animation, music, and interesting story.

## 3. Spirited Away

Spirited Away is about a young girl who enters a mysterious spirit world and tries to find a way to save her parents. I like its creative world and unique characters.

## 4. A Silent Voice

A Silent Voice is about a boy who tries to make up for his past mistakes and reconnect with a girl he once bullied. I like its meaningful story about friendship, kindness, and forgiveness.

## 5. Weathering with You

Weathering with You is about a boy who meets a girl with the ability to control the weather. I like its beautiful animation, music, and emotional story.
